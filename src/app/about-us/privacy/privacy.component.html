Privacy Policy

At GOVocal, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

- Personal information (name, email) when you sign up.

- Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve the GOVocal platform.
- To ensure a safe and respectful community environment.
- To communicate updates, security alerts, and account-related information.

3. How We Protect Your Data

- We use encryption and security measures to safeguard your data.
- Your data is not sold to third parties.
- Users can request data deletion by contacting support.

4. Cookies and Tracking

- We use cookies to improve user experience and analytics.
- You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Updates to This Policy

- GOVocal may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
- Users will be notified of significant changes.
